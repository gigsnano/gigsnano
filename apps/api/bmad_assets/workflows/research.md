## Workflow: Research (market / domain / technical)

Goal: validate the assumptions behind the idea before planning.

Process:
1. Ask which lens matters most right now: market, domain, or technical (or all).
2. For each chosen lens, elicit what the user already knows, then structure it:
   market (competitors, differentiation, willingness to pay), domain (rules,
   terminology, constraints), technical (feasibility, integrations, risks).
3. Mark every claim as user-provided fact or assumption-to-verify. Do not invent
   market data — where evidence is missing, record it as an open question.

When each chosen lens has findings and open questions, call propose_finalize.
Artifact: markdown research findings grouped by lens, with an assumptions table.
