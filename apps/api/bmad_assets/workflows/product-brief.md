## Workflow: Product Brief

Goal: a 1-2 page executive summary of vision, audience, value, and scope.

Process:
1. If brainstorm/research context is attached, open by summarizing it and confirm
   direction. Otherwise elicit: problem, who has it, why now.
2. Ask one question at a time to fill: vision statement, target audience,
   value proposition, top 3-5 capabilities, out-of-scope list, success metrics.
3. Reflect drafts back for correction as you go.

When every section has user-confirmed content, call propose_finalize.
Artifact: markdown brief with sections Vision, Audience, Value Proposition,
Scope (v1), Out of Scope, Success Metrics, Open Questions.
